A personal Android travel journal

Your travels, remembered.

Where to next? is a personal travel journal for Android phones. Explore a world map, record the places you have visited, and keep your memories and photos together.

A journal for your journeys

Record countries and attractions, add your own places, and collect traveller levels and achievements as your journal grows. Use the app in portrait or landscape.

Ready to travel offline

The bundled country map and journal work without an internet connection. Online map detail and attraction images are available when connected.

Optional Google Drive sync

Your journal and personal photos stay on your phone. If you connect Google Drive from the Sync menu, the app also saves journal data and photos in your Google account’s app-data storage so you can restore them later. Google authorization is optional.

Read the privacy policy for details about data, permissions, and deletion.

Made for personal use

Developed by Stephanos as a personal gift, Where to next? is distributed to its intended user through a Google Play testing invitation. This website provides information about the app; it does not provide access to anyone’s journal.
