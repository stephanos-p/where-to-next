A personal Android travel journal

Privacy policy

Where to next? is a personal Android travel journal developed by Stephanos. This policy explains how the app and this information website handle data.

Last updated: 10 September 2026.

Journal data on your phone

The app stores visited countries and attractions, custom places, descriptions, visit dates, photo references, ordering, traveller progress, achievements, and settings locally. Personal photos selected for import are resized and copied into app-private storage. A local recovery snapshot also stores journal state; it is not a separate backup of the photo files.

Photos and location permissions

Photo-library access lets you browse and select pictures to add to your journal. The Google Photos selection flow may use Google’s online services to retrieve the pictures you select. Selected pictures are copied into the journal; the app does not upload your entire photo library.

When available and permitted, device location can set the initial map position. Location is optional and the map has a fallback. The app saves the avatar’s map position, which may initially reflect your device location; that saved position is included in journal sync. The app does not continuously track your device’s location in the background.

Google Drive access and use

If you choose Sync → Connect, Google asks you to authorize access to this app’s own Drive app-data storage using the drive.appdata permission. The app uploads and downloads journal state and referenced personal photos to support sync and restoration. It also uses technical sync information such as installation identifiers, revisions, timestamps, and photo integrity hashes.

The permission does not give this app general access to your ordinary Drive documents. Google handles account authorization. Access tokens are used by the native Android integration to make authorized requests to Google; the app does not ask for or store your Google password.

Google user data is used only to provide journal sync and recovery. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Where to next?’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Services that receive requests

There is no developer-operated server receiving your journal or photos. When sync is enabled, Google processes the journal and photos in the connected account. Optional online map detail and attraction images send ordinary network requests, including your IP address and requested resources, to map providers and Wikimedia image servers. These requests can reveal the map areas or attraction images being requested.

This website uses GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub processes visitor network information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website adds no analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts. The Android app has no advertising or analytics integration.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

You can edit journal entries and remove photos in the app. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes the app’s local data; Android device backup and restore settings may affect retained device backups. Keep a verified recoverable copy before doing this if you want to preserve your memories.

Sync → Disconnect stops app sync but does not delete local data or previously saved Drive data, and does not itself revoke Google authorization. You can revoke access through your Google Account’s third-party connections settings.

For cloud deletion, disconnect sync first, then use Google Drive’s Settings → Manage apps controls to delete this app’s hidden app data where offered. Revoking access or uninstalling alone should not be treated as deleting the cloud copy. Older sync generations and photo files may remain in app-data storage after you edit or delete an individual item locally. Contact the developer if you need help removing stored data.

Security and changes

Google Drive requests use HTTPS, and local personal photos use Android app-private storage. No storage or sync system guarantees against all loss. Check successful sync and recovery before relying on a backup. This policy will be updated when data practices change.
