Using the app

Use the app to record your own travel experiences and add content you have permission to use. The map is an exploration aid, not a navigation service or an authoritative statement of borders or travel conditions.

Your content

You retain your rights in the journal entries and photos you add. The app processes that content to provide local journaling and, if you choose to connect Google Drive, sync and restoration. See the privacy policy for details.

Availability and backups

The app is a personal project provided without a service-level commitment. Errors, interruptions, changes to third-party services, or loss of device data can affect availability and recovery. Confirm successful sync and test restoration before clearing data or changing devices. Choosing a version during a sync conflict replaces the other version rather than merging the two.

Third-party services

Google Play, Google Drive, photo providers, and online map and image providers operate under their own terms. Google Drive sync is optional and requires a working Google account, authorization, connectivity, and available storage.
