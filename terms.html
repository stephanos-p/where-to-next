Terms of use

Where to next? is provided by Stephanos as a personal Android travel journal for its intended invited user.

Last updated: 23 September 2026.

Using the app

Use the app to record your own travel experiences and add content you have permission to use. The map is an exploration aid, not a navigation service or an authoritative statement of borders or travel conditions.

Your content

You retain your rights in the journal entries and photos you select. The app creates compressed display copies of selected images and stores them locally. Optional personal cloud sync uploads the journal and those copies to a private server so another Android phone can restore them. See the privacy policy for details.

Availability and backups

The app is a personal project provided without a service-level commitment. Errors, interruptions, third-party service changes, or loss of device data can affect availability and recovery. Confirm successful sync and test restoration before clearing data or changing devices. Choosing a version during a sync conflict replaces the other version rather than merging the two. The separate server recovery backup stores an encrypted archive in Google Drive; a completed independent restore rehearsal is still pending.

Third-party services

Google Play, netcup Webhosting, Google Drive, photo providers, and online map and image providers operate under their own terms. Personal cloud sync requires a working server account, connectivity, and available storage. The Google Drive connection is used by the operator's encrypted backup tool, not by the Android app.

These terms may be updated to reflect changes to the app. Nothing here limits rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law. Questions can be sent to Stephanos at papastylianou.stephanos@gmail.com.
